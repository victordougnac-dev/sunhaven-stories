CREDITS

SUNHAVEN STORIES

An original 3D open-world browser game built with Three.js, TypeScript, procedural PBR shaders, and Web Audio synthesis — tailored for CrazyGames.

  • World Design: 12 distinct districts across Sunhaven
  • Interiors: 14 fully furnished enterable buildings & underground areas
  • Fleet: Sports cars, sedans, SUVs, trucks, motorcycles, speedboats, prop planes, private jets & helicopters
  • Secrets: 16 original hidden Easter eggs to discover
